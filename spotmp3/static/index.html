    Connect Spotify

    Lets people pick from their own Spotify playlists and Liked Songs, and get playlists longer than 100 tracks. Pasting links works without this.

    1. Open the Spotify developer dashboard, sign in and press Create app. Any name and description will do. Tick Web API.
    2. Under Redirect URIs, add this address exactly, then save:
    3. Open the app's Settings in the dashboard, copy the Client ID and paste it below.
    4. While the Spotify app is in Development Mode, add the Spotify email of everyone who will use it under User Management.

    No client secret is needed. SpotMP3 signs in to Spotify with PKCE in the browser, so there is no secret to store or leak.
